This page explains how admin access is decided on SokoPlus. It is written for admins and for the people who set them up. For the technical detail, see **Permissions & roles** under the developer docs.

## The starting point: role defaults

Every admin account has a role, and every role comes with a **default set of abilities**:

| Role | In one sentence |
|------|-----------------|
| **Partner Admin** | Runs day-to-day operations — users, vendors, orders, categories and support. |
| **Major Admin** | Everything a Partner Admin does, plus regional management and the ability to set up Partner Admins. |
| **Super Admin** | Full control, including the permission system, regions and system health. |

When you create a new admin, they can do whatever their role allows — you do not have to switch anything on by hand.

## Adjusting one admin

Sometimes one person needs a little more, or a little less, than their role. Open their row and you will see three choices for each ability:

- **Inherit** — use the role default. This is the normal state.
- **Allow** — give this one admin the ability, even if the role does not have it.
- **Deny** — take the ability away from this one admin, even if the role has it.

<Callout type="note">
**Deny always wins.** If a role allows something but you deny it for one admin, that admin does not have it. To undo a deny, set the ability back to **Inherit**.
</Callout>

Changes are collected as you edit and only take effect when you press **Save** — nothing is written until then.

## The grant matrix

The grant matrix answers one question: **who is allowed to hand out which ability?**

- A **Super Admin** can grant anything.
- A **Major Admin** and **Partner Admin** can grant the everyday abilities, but **not** the three platform controls: managing permissions, managing regions, and moving accounts between regions.
- These three stay with the **Super Admin** only. This is what stops delegation from quietly becoming escalation.

If you try to grant something your role cannot hand out, that choice is simply skipped — the rest of your changes still save. The **Grant Matrix** tab in the Permissions screen shows you exactly what your role may grant.

## Region scope

Some admins are tied to a **region**. A region-scoped admin only sees and acts on accounts in their region. Moving an account to a different region is a platform control (the `assign_regions` ability), so it is normally reserved for Super Admins.

## Every change is recorded

Any administrative action that changes data asks for a **reason** and is saved with it. The reason is attached to the action so a manager can later see *why* something happened, not just *what* happened. Only a Super Admin can act without a reason, and even then the action is marked as having skipped the reason.
