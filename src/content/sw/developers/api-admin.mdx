Endpoints za msimamizi zinasimamia shughuli za jukwaa. Endpoints za mfumo, fedha na rekodi za msimamizi mkuu pekee ziko kwenye ukurasa wa **API ya msimamizi mkuu**.

## Takwimu za jukwaa

| Method | Path | Auth | Maelezo |
|--------|------|------|-------------|
| GET | `/admin/stats` | partnerAdmin+ | Idadi za watumiaji/biashara/maagizo/mapato |
| GET | `/admin/chart-data` | partnerAdmin+ | Data ya chati ya mwezi |

## Ruhusa na mikoa

| Method | Path | Auth | Maelezo |
|--------|------|------|-------------|
| GET | `/admin/permissions/grant-matrix` | partnerAdmin+ | Funguo ambazo mtendaji huyu anaweza kutoa; `superAdmin` hupata pia matrix kamili ya jukumu × funguo |
| GET | `/admin/permissions/role/:role` | partnerAdmin+ | Chaguo-msingi za jukumu (`partnerAdmin` au `majorAdmin`) |
| PUT | `/admin/permissions/role/:role` | partnerAdmin+ | Badilisha chaguo-msingi za jukumu |
| GET | `/admin/permissions/:adminId` | partnerAdmin+ | Seti yenye nguvu ya msimamizi mmoja: `enabled`, `effect` (`grant`/`deny`/null) na `grantable` kwa kila funguo |
| PUT | `/admin/permissions/:adminId` | partnerAdmin+ | Andika mabadiliko ya msimamizi mmoja. `effect: 'grant'`/`'deny'` huandika safu; `effect: null` huifuta (hurithi chaguo-msingi la jukumu) |
| POST | `/admin/regions/assign` | majorAdmin+ | Hamisha mtumiaji au majorAdmin kwenda mkoa. Inadhibitiwa na `assign_regions`, **sababu inahitajika** |

> **Dokezo:** Ugawaji una mipaka: `majorAdmin`/`partnerAdmin` hawawezi kutoa au kuondoa `manage_permissions`, `manage_regions` au `assign_regions`; funguo hizo huachwa kwenye ombi na hurudishwa kwenye safu ya `rejected`. Kuweka mkoa kunahitaji `regionId` na (kwa wasio superAdmin) lazima kubaki ndani ya mkoa wa mtendaji mwenyewe.

## Msingi wa usimamizi

| Method | Path | Auth | Maelezo |
|--------|------|------|---------|
| GET | `/admin/notes` | `manage_notes` | Madokezo ya `targetType` + `targetId` moja |
| POST | `/admin/notes` | msimamizi | Unda dokezo au jibu (maudhui ni sababu ya ukaguzi) |
| PUT | `/admin/notes/:id` | msimamizi | Hariri dokezo lako mwenyewe |
| DELETE | `/admin/notes/:id` | msimamizi | Futa dokezo lako kwa upole; `superAdmin` anaweza kufuta lolote |
| GET | `/admin/flags` | `manage_flags` | Orodhesha bendera (lengo, hali, ukali, ukurasa, kikomo) |
| POST | `/admin/flags` | msimamizi | Ibua bendera — sababu inahitajika, ukali `low`/`medium`/`high`/`critical` |
| PUT | `/admin/flags/:id/status` | msimamizi | Hali yoyote isiyo ya mwisho inaweza kuhamia hali yoyote; `resolved`/`dismissed` ni za mwisho |
| GET | `/admin/tasks/mine` | `manage_tasks` | Kazi zilizokabidhiwa kwako |
| GET | `/admin/tasks` | `manage_tasks` | Kazi zote, zinazochujika |
| POST | `/admin/tasks` | `assign_tasks` | Unda na ukabidhi kazi kwa msimamizi |
| GET | `/admin/tasks/:id` | `manage_tasks` | Kazi pamoja na madokezo yake ya ndani |
| POST | `/admin/tasks/:id/transition` | `manage_tasks` | Sogeza kazi; dokezo linahitajika |
| GET | `/admin/audit` | `manage_activity_log` | Ufuatiliaji wa ukaguzi wa kipengele |

Hali za kazi: `OPEN` → `IN_PROGRESS` → `REVIEW` → `DONE` → `CONFIRMED`, na `CANCELLED` kutoka hali yoyote isiyo ya mwisho. Mpokeaji husogeza hatua mbili za kwanza; mkabidhi huweka done, huthibitisha au hughairi. `superAdmin` anaweza kufanya mabadiliko yoyote halali.

> **Dokezo:** Madokezo yanakubali `visibility: "internal"` pekee. Bendera na kazi zote zinaunga mkono viungo vya kipengele; kuunda kazi na `flagId` huunganisha bendera na kazi pande zote. Kila mabadiliko hukaguliwa kwa uwezo na sababu.
