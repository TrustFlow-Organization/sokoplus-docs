Uploads hudhibitiwa na `multer` (in-memory, kikomo cha faili **5 MB**) na kupelekwa kwenye Cloudinary. Endpoint zote zinahitaji **JWT**.

## Upload / KYC (`/api/upload`)

| Method | Path | Auth | Maelezo |
|--------|------|------|-------------|
| POST | `/upload/selfie` | JWT | Pakia picha ya selfie — multipart, field `file` |
| POST | `/upload/document` | JWT | Pakia hati (KYC) — multipart, field `file` |
| POST | `/upload/chat` | JWT | Pakia picha ya mazungumzo — multipart, field `file` |

Kila ombi hupokea faili moja hadi **5 MB**. Selfie na picha za mazungumzo hupokea `image/*`; hati za KYC hupokea `image/*` au `application/pdf`. Zaidi ya faili moja au faili kubwa hurudisha `400`.

<Callout type="note">
Kwa uthibitisho wa KYC (selfie, hati, hati ya biashara) kila faili huingizwa moja kwa moja. Pakia selfie kwanza, kisha hati.
</Callout>

Majibu yanarudisha URL ya Cloudinary na ujumbe:

```json
{ "url": "https://res.cloudinary.com/…/selfie.jpg", "message": "Selfie uploaded" }
```
